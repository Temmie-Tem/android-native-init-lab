current-boot limit remains one hour and sixteen named actions. Each action has

to remaining lease time immediately before OPEN. Existing locks serialize
actions and recovery. No additional activation JSON or per-query approval is
introduced; the fresh F1 binding pins this complete execution closure.

Guard failure, expired lease or uncertain action cannot reactivate a session.
Only the originally approved exact rollback remains. After rollback and health,
the close summary is rederived from the bound lease/result receipts. Exploration
PASS needs initial proof, at least one successful named action, no pending or
failed action, and verified rollback/health. Initial USB proof alone is not
named exploration proof. Missing or malformed action evidence yields NO_PROOF
without blocking rollback. No automatic recovery or indefinite residency is
claimed.
