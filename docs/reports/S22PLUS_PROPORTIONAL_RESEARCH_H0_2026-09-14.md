# S22+ proportional research amendment — H0

The operator requested faster, freer experiments, including spontaneous
in-scope suggestions, and approved applying the proposed amendment. Revision
11 updates the common contract, its pinned details, risk tiers, Process v2 and
the S22+ target contract together. The exact selected target remains
`SM-S906N/g0q/S906NKSS7FYG8`.

The [new policy](../operations/S22PLUS_PROPORTIONAL_RESEARCH_V1.md) is implemented
through the shared native-baseline owner. One finite task grant names the
target, admitted N, original A, operations, runtime profiles, functional source
scope and recovery mode. Later candidate manifests are selected by machine
checks without another human approval. Explicit task budgets replace the old
one-E/600-second limit in this mode. Attended and deferred physical recovery
remain distinct; deferred recovery accepts unknown powered activity while
waiting for later attended A, without claiming automatic failure recovery.

New candidate declarations use the existing typed-evidence/static factory.
Candidate data and unrelated other-target root records do not invalidate the
new capability review. Hazard-relevant machinery and selected policy remain
bound. Changed executable fragments require a focused, reusable independent
assessment of their exact bytes and native context; a permitted filename alone
does not prove absence of new privileged or hardware effects. Retained N and
eligible repeat E keep their original verified artifacts and source provenance.

A prospective E gains repeat eligibility only after its first new-mode global
claim, E health and healthy N return are proved. Later repeats have new intents
and keep the original claim active. Historical consumed E images remain
ineligible. A recovered Android terminal after a failed N return does not mint
repeat eligibility. Interrupted H0 selections can be completed or cancelled
without new approval; cancellation returns only proved unused capacity and
never reuses an ordinal or conceals a device reservation.

The fixed D0 observer records a fresh bounded attempt after a supported read
failure, preserving old evidence and authentication history. It sends no
CONTROL or caller-selected command. New health requires authenticated fixed
reads, fresh same-boot state, DETACH, descriptor close and guard release. Only
the new mode accepts this observation tail; an unresolved F1 owner cannot be
released by D0. The exact earlier OPEN/RX-zero stop has an immutable import
reader, without replaying its old caller or reopening the consumed F1 grant.
