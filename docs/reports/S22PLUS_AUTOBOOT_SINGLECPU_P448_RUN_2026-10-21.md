## What the retained evidence proves

D0 captured `last_kmsg` once, with unchanged before/after health. Its 16 bounded
raw captures pass, including the 2097136-byte log. The unchanged strict collector
accepts 131 transition records from the first candidate boot, rejects one

The first kernel span contains the exact `cpuidle.off=1 maxcpus=1` arguments
and reports one activated processor. Authenticated preparation reaches
`STORAGE_READY`, with both fixed helper exits successful. Source-bound transition
records then establish:

- First file lifetime: checked unmount at 8.641 seconds, partition RO at 8.642,
  clean-superblock observation at 8.643, RAM receipt at 8.646, and worker result
  zero at 8.647 seconds.
- Automatic entry: second-admission BEGIN at 8.693 seconds, later normal RW
  mount RETURN zero at 15.222 seconds, and installed actual-init identity
  check RETURN zero at 15.387 seconds.
- rcS: successful fork at 15.448 seconds, checked fork-boundary record, first
  wait return, descriptor cleanup/probe, and child exec BEGIN at 15.451 seconds.
  An exec attempt alone is not successful rcS execution.

The noncanonical line contains one exact MAC-valid frame plus a firmware-text
suffix. An H0-only extent audit authenticates that frame separately and joins
it to the original parent's sequence, executable, boot and sleep BEGIN. It
records `RCS_SLEEP_FIRST RETURN 0` at 15.461 seconds. The saved errno snapshot
is 9; successful syscall return governs the result and the decoder's error is
null. This separate positive fact is not merged into the original collector,
and neither its rejected-line count nor the live terminal is relabelled.

No rcS reaped-success/completion record, 120-second runtime chain or SSH proof
is present. The optional proc observer has no samples: this rcS invocation
starts after its 12.5-second opportunity window. That absence does not establish
an absent watchdog thread or failed hardware pet. The revision-6 persistent
runtime file has not been retrieved, so its fifteen sampled-state proof is
not claimed from this retained log.

The first retained kernel span's last timestamp is 15.494647 seconds. A warm-
reset firmware boundary follows. The next span again reports the selected
CPU arguments and one processor, and independently authenticates preparation
under a different boot identity. Its final authenticated preparation record is
`MODULE_BEGIN ordinal=76 name=ufs_qcom`; completion and child settlement are
not proved. A second warm-reset firmware boundary follows this span.
Missing returns do not locate a stall or prove that later work did not execute.

No watchdog bark/bite, kernel panic or Oops line is present in the selected
retained kernel spans. This is an observation of the retained range, not proof
of their absence at runtime. The exact reset times, causes and relationship
to the operator's physical recovery sequence remain unknown. In particular,
these records do not establish that the first reset happened at 15.5 seconds
or that either reset was caused by the watchdog.

PID1 is positively observed. The required usable service/SSH outcome was not
established. This experiment neither proves a watchdog fix nor identifies the
remaining cause. Original-A recovery is complete; native_data's current
consistency and its current-run early/runtime files remain unknown.
