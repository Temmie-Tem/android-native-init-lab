# S22+ deferred physical native-baseline recovery — H0

## Change and evidence limit

The operator requested a contract and operating change after repeated normal
N -> E -> N closure. The selected target is only SM-S906N/g0q/S906NKSS7FYG8.
The new [common-incorporated policy](../operations/S22PLUS_NATIVE_BASELINE_DEFERRED_RECOVERY_V1.md)
allows one explicitly selected native-origin V2 experiment without attendance,
with a maximum one reservation and 600 BOOTTIME seconds. Normal execution
retains its existing proof before E installation and normal N restoration.
Unexpected failure stops further effects and defers the original Android A
recovery until the operator can attend.

This is an explicit permanent-boundary change to attendance and recovery timing.
It accepts an unresolved powered device with unknown activity and potentially
delayed notification. It does not establish quiescence, automatic kernel/USB
failure recovery, indefinite battery safety or continuous native health. The
Conditional Autonomous F1 definition remains unchanged. No device command,
new request/grant, candidate build, partition effect or live unattended trial
was performed for this H0 change.

## Implementation and compatibility

The shared baseline owner adds only the explicit request mode
`deferred-physical-v1`, its distinct source-bound review and mode-matching grant.
Absent-mode V1/V2 records retain their exact schema and attendance meaning.
The grant records the actual supplied attendance flag rather than fabricating
attendance. Bootstrap, Android-origin/direct-exit operation and larger operation
sets cannot select the mode. Existing target ownership, source/artifact identity,
fresh authentication, global E consumption and per-role no-replay remain intact.

Caught failure records STOP and grant closure, publishes `PARKED`, reports
unknown device activity and returns before the recovery branch. It does not
discover a recovery endpoint, authenticate native or transfer A. Ownership
remains held. Missing terminal after a process cut is `UNRESOLVED`, with both
process and device activity unknown; status alone cannot authorize recovery.

Later `recover --attended` retains the original A and frozen recovery closure,
including the new policy and risk authority. Expiry does not renew research or
prevent the already bound recovery. Missing/uncertain A completion forbids
replay; proven A completion permits only exact rooted-FYG8 final health. Fully
proved native reporting cuts remain H0 repair without a new transition.

root contract advances to Revision 10 with its updated details pin. Risk tiers,
target adoption and Process-v2 routing identify the exception. V2's original

`653aba9bdd29ddf0f26487755c3a0c9ca3c1fae50bbeff8b73ebffacb4c2c8fd`.
