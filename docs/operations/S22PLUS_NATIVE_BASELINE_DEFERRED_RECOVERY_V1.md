## Explicit common exception and accepted failure model

This separately common-incorporated permanent-boundary exception changes
attendance and the timing of failure recovery for one resident-baseline V2
experiment. The operator requested this operating change after the closed
P388/P389/P390/P391 normal N -> E -> N runs. Those runs support the normal
machine-controlled path; they do not prove recovery from every failure.

Only a fresh V2 request with `execution_mode=deferred-physical-v1` selects this
exception. An absent mode retains the original V1/V2 interpretation and exact
record fields. Old grants, approvals, journals, policy bytes and consumed roles
are never repurposed. This exception expressly replaces the V2 attendance rule
and the common requirement for immediate rollback within this exact mode. It
does not satisfy or alter Conditional Autonomous F1's automatic-recovery test.

The operator may be absent during normal N -> E -> N. An unexpected failure
closes research and defers physical recovery until the operator can attend.
The accepted residual state may be powered N, E or Download with unknown device
activity, display, charging and battery consumption; no bounded unattended
residence, hardware quiescence or automatic kernel/USB-stall recovery is proved.
The finite host deadline blocks new dispatch, but does not promise to stop an
in-flight kernel/device effect. Host death, power loss or an unavailable client
may delay notification. These limits are part of the reviewable exact request's
bound policy, not a claim that stopping the host makes the device safe or healthy.
Known new time-sensitive hazards or lost physical recovery block a new run.

## Failure and later recovery

The existing target lease, durable F1 owner, global E content claim, exclusive
role intents, raw capture and descriptor/transport guards remain mandatory.
Normal N restoration requires full E proof and its exact timely Download return;
an experiment failure never authorizes a new E or N attempt.

On a caught failure, the owner records the research stop, closes the grant and
publishes `deferred-recovery.json` as `PARKED` with device activity `UNKNOWN` and
recovery still required. It emits an immediate visible message if the process
and client remain available. It performs no recovery endpoint discovery,
native authentication or Android transfer in that branch. Ownership remains
held and all later research is blocked. `PARKED` is not a healthy terminal.

After an abrupt host/process cut, durable reservation/intent/owner records still
prevent replay. H0 status reports an unfinished operation as `UNRESOLVED` when
no stop was recorded; the process and device may still be active. It must not
infer failure, quiescence or permission for concurrent recovery from an absent
terminal. Partial reservation/owner publication needs H0 reconciliation before
any further effect; an unreadable record is a stop, never renewed capacity.

Later recovery requires actual attendance and the original operation, target
lease, exact recovery closure and current physical Download binding. It retains
the existing checks against descriptor/transfer overlap and does not renew the
expired grant. Only the originally bound A may transfer, at most once. A durable
A intent with uncertain completion forbids replay. Proven A completion permits
only the existing exact rooted-FYG8 final-health continuation. Only a fully
validated terminal can release ownership. A source change while parked requires
restoring the bound recovery inputs or a separately reviewed scoped repair;
an edited grant, erased intent or bypassed pin is never recovery authority.

Complete retained raw/close proof of an already finished native terminal permits
H0 publication and owner-release repair. It must not trigger another transition.
The original N admission, E outcome, normal N restoration, Android return and
final health remain separate evidence. A successful N terminal is past health,
not continuous liveness or standing permission for another operation.
